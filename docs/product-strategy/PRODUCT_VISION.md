# REGrow — Product Vision

**Product:** REGrow by INFYN Group — the Real Estate Growth Operating System · **Date:** 2026-11-16
(INFYN Group is the company brand; REGrow is the product brand.)

## Positioning statement
**For** Indian real-estate developers and the independent channel-partner agencies who sell for them,
**who** lose sales because leads, follow-ups, inventory and partner deals live in disconnected tools,
**REGrow** is a modular real-estate growth operating system
**that** makes sure every enquiry is acted on in minutes, every deal is registered fairly, and every unit sold is tracked to cash.
**Unlike** horizontal CRMs (Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce) that need heavy customisation, and real-estate suites (Sell.Do, LeadSquared)
built around the developer alone, **REGrow** proves response speed and treats agencies as independent organisations with
protected ownership of their own customers.

**Tagline candidates (to test):** "No lead left waiting." · "Every lead. Every partner. Every unit." · "Grow sales, not spreadsheets."

## Customer problems (ranked by evidence)
1. **Leads leak** — slow or no response to paid leads; no visibility of who sat on what. *(Customer #1's explicit pain; widely reported speed-to-lead research — attribution of the "21× in 5 minutes" figure is disputed, cite carefully.)*
2. **Follow-ups are forgotten** — no enforced next step after each call.
3. **Channel conflict** — first-tag vs direct-enquiry disputes; developers and agencies don't trust each other's records (validity windows of 30/60/90 days are typical — vendor sources, M).
4. **Agencies duplicate work** across many developer portals.
5. **Inventory, bookings and collections** are manual and disconnected from sales.
6. **Owners lack a single view** of marketing ROI → sales → collections.
7. **Tool sprawl** — multiple subscriptions that don't talk.

## Segments and the order we serve them
| Order | Segment | Entry product | Expansion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Developers, 5–30 sales staff, paid lead gen (Hyderabad first) | REGrow Leads | Sales → Operations → Partner Network |
| 2 | CP agencies working with those developers | Free invited workspace → REGrow for Agencies | Multi-developer registrations, commissions |
| 3 | Mid-size developers (30–100 seats, multi-city) | REGrow Sales + Operations | Enterprise features |
| Later | Large developers, brokerage networks, individual agents | Enterprise / Agent app | — |

Individual agents: only commercially viable as part of an agency or as a free network participant — not a standalone paid segment in 2027.

## Product families (validated with adjustments)
1. **CRM & Sales** — *REGrow Leads* is carved out as the entry product (capture, routing, SLA, follow-ups); the rest of CRM & Sales builds on it.
2. **Real Estate Operations** — projects, inventory, holds/bookings, collections (largely built; Phase 2 parked).
3. **Partner Network** (renamed from "Channel Partner Network" — shorter, covers agencies and individual brokers).
4. **REGrow Intelligence** — split as briefed: **Business Intelligence** (included opinionated dashboards; advanced as add-on) and **Market & Buyer Intelligence** (postponed; consent- and licence-based only).
5. **Automation & Integrations** — horizontal platform capability under all families, monetised only through usage (messages, minutes, API volume), never by paywalling basic automation.

## Non-negotiable principles (from the founders' brief)
Module entitlements ≠ user roles ≠ cross-organisation permissions. Developers own projects and inventory. Agencies are independent
organisations and own their CRM. Lead ownership stays with the creating organisation. Deal registration is a project-specific
commercial record, not a transfer of the lead. Agencies see only explicitly shared projects. Enforcement server-side **and** at the database layer.
Any future lead marketplace is opt-in, permission-controlled and commercially separate.

## Three-year picture (direction, not commitment)
- **2027:** REGrow Leads + Sales in Telangana and one more metro; 50–100 developer customers; agencies joining via invitations.
- **2028:** Partner Network becomes the moat — agencies run their business on REGrow across many developers; Operations module widely adopted.
- **2029:** Intelligence products on licensed data; enterprise tier; regional expansion (UAE corridor is common for Indian developers — to be researched).
